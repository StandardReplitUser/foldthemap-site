# Fold the Map: Support
**Contact:** [Open a support request](https://github.com/StandardReplitUser/foldthemap-site/issues). We aim to reply within 3 business days.
*Draft, 2026-10-14. This page will be the App Store "Support URL" (Apple Guideline 1.5 requires an easy way to contact us).*

## FAQ
**How do I play?** Swipe across the map to fold it. Matching landmarks that land on each other merge into the next tier: campfire → tent → … → capital city. Reach the goal shown at the top of the level.
**I'm stuck.** Every level has a solution. Use **Hint** to see the next fold, or **Undo** to step back. *(Make sure this is true at launch.)*
**Is my progress saved?** Yes, on this device only. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between devices, and deleting the game deletes progress.
**I bought Remove Ads. How do I restore it?** Go to Settings → **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account. Remove Ads turns off all automatic ads. Optional "watch an ad for a hint" buttons stay available but are never required.
**How do I request a refund?** Purchases are handled by Apple: https://support.apple.com/118223 (Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple). *(Check the link before publishing.)*
**I saw an inappropriate ad.** Email us with the time and a screenshot, and we'll block it in AdMob.
**How do I stop personalised ads?** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## Reporting a bug
Send the device model, iOS version, game version (shown in Settings) and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps.
