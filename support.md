## FAQ
**How do I play?** Swipe across the map to fold it. Matching landmarks that land on each other merge into the next tier: campfire → tent → … → capital city. Reach the goal shown at the top of the level.

**I'm stuck.** Every level can be solved. Use **Undo** to step back or **Reset** to start the level again. From level 6 on (and in the Daily Atlas), **Hint** shows the next fold; the first levels teach the moves step by step. Up to level 20, the first hint in each level is a free teaching hint (marked FREE): it doesn't cost anything and you can still earn 3 stars. Any other hint caps that solve at 2 stars.

**Where do hints come from?** The daily top-up, chests on the map, Daily Atlas streak rewards (one missed day a week is forgiven, so your streak keeps going), a one-time free hint in your first session (no video needed), hint packs in the Shop, and an optional short video ad for a hint (up to 5 a day). You never have to buy anything to finish a level.

**Is my progress saved?** Yes, on this device only. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between devices, and deleting the game deletes progress.

**What does Remove Ads do?** Remove Ads = no ads between levels + unlimited hints. You never need the "watch a video for a hint" button again, and the game doesn't start Google's ad software at all. As with any hint other than the free teaching hint, a solve that used one earns up to 2 stars.

**I bought Remove Ads. How do I restore it?** In the game, open Settings → **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Hint packs are used up when you use them, so they aren't restored.

**How do I request a refund?** Purchases are handled by Apple: [Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple](https://support.apple.com/118223).

**I saw an inappropriate ad.** The game only asks for ads suitable for all ages. If one isn't, tap the ⓘ (AdChoices) icon on the ad to report it to Google, or email us at [jurbanstudio@mail.grokbot.com](mailto:jurbanstudio@mail.grokbot.com) with the time and a screenshot, and we'll block it in AdMob.

**Why can't I watch a video for a hint?** In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads only load after you've answered Google's consent message. If you haven't answered it, the game tells you to use Settings → **Privacy choices**. If the game couldn't check your choice (for example, you were offline), connect and tap Hint again. Hint packs, the daily top-up and chests work either way.

**How do I stop personalized ads?** iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can also change your ad consent in the game: Settings → **Privacy choices**. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
